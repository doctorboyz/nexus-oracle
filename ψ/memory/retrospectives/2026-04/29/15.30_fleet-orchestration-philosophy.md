## Lessons Learned

1. **Drive-to-completion, not send-and-wait**: When human sets a goal, PM dispatches work, monitors, verifies each step's KPIs, then proceeds. Don't send a message and passively wait.

2. **Proxy metrics ≠ real goal**: WR, PF, MaxDD are proxies. The real goal is "profit consistently without blowing up." When proxy and goal conflict, the goal wins.

3. **maw ollama = real session**: Don't treat `running: ollama` as inferior. It's an active Claude session that can receive and process messages.
